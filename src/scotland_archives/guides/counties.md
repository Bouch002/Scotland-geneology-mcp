# Scottish Counties — Genealogy Reference

Historical Scottish counties as used in genealogy records, OPR registers,
census returns, and ScotlandsPeople searches. Note that county boundaries
changed in 1975 (regional reorganisation) and again in 1996 (unitary authorities).
Genealogy records almost always use the pre-1975 counties.

## Notes on Searching by County

**ScotlandsPeople county filter:** Use the historical county name as listed above.
Some variations are accepted; if a county search returns no results, try the alternative name.

**OPR and county:** OPR registers are indexed by parish, not county. Use the parish
list within ScotlandsPeople to browse parishes within a given county.

**Census and county:** Census records are searchable by county. For large counties
(Lanarkshire, Perthshire, Inverness-shire), refine with a parish or town name.

**Gaelic counties:** In Gaelic-speaking areas (Inverness-shire, Ross and Cromarty,
Argyllshire, parts of Perthshire and Sutherland), personal names and place names
in records may appear in Gaelic or in inconsistent Anglicisations.

## Post-1975 Regions (for reference)

Local government reorganisation in 1975 replaced the 33 counties with 9 regions
and 3 island areas. These do **not** appear in genealogy records but are used by
modern archives:

- Highland (covers former Inverness-shire, Ross & Cromarty, Sutherland, Caithness, Nairnshire)
- Strathclyde (covers Lanarkshire, Ayrshire, Renfrewshire, Dunbartonshire, Argyllshire, Buteshire)
- Lothian (covers Midlothian, East Lothian, West Lothian)
- Central (covers Stirlingshire, Clackmannanshire, Kinross-shire)
- Tayside (covers Perthshire, Angus, Kincardineshire)
- Grampian (covers Aberdeenshire, Banffshire, Moray, Nairnshire)
- Borders (covers Berwickshire, Roxburghshire, Selkirkshire, Peeblesshire)
- Dumfries & Galloway (covers Dumfriesshire, Kirkcudbrightshire, Wigtownshire)
- Fife (unchanged)
- Western Isles / Eilean Siar (island authority)
- Orkney Islands (island authority)
- Shetland Islands (island authority)
